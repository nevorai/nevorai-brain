# Nevorai Bookings: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    A booking page like Calendly: share a link, the customer picks a slot, both get the confirmation and reminders.

    ## Who it is for
    Coaches, consultants, clinics, agencies, anyone who takes meetings or appointments.

    ## How it works
    Set your hours and meeting types; share bookings.nevorai.com/yourname; bookings land on your calendar page and in Nevorai Connect.

    ## How to start
    Sign up at https://bookings.nevorai.com; the Pro plan is at billing.nevorai.com.

    ## What people use it for
    - Demo calls from a WhatsApp chat
- Consultations and appointments
- Class or trial-session slots
