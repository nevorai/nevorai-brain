# Nevorai Agencies: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    The software that runs a marketing or media agency: sales (projects, leads, sales team), HR (records, attendance, salary and payslips, joining and exit, letters, departments, roles), clients and brands, content (calendar, tasks, shoots, reels, workflows), production desks and dashboards.

    ## Who it is for
    Marketing, media, content and creative agencies with 5 to 200 people.

    ## How it works
    One login for the whole agency; each department has its own tabs; owners see every dashboard; every new feature for the year is included.

    ## How to start
    Ask the team for a demo. The Complete plan is yearly; see products/agencies/pricing.md.

    ## What people use it for
    - Replace spreadsheets for HR, salary and attendance
- Client, brand and content pipeline in one place
- Sales pipeline and team targets
