# Nevorai Tasks: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    A simple to-do app with four boxes (urgent and important), notes, sheets, files and AI credits.

    ## Who it is for
    Founders and small teams who want one list that is actually used.

    ## How it works
    Add a task, drop it in a box, tick it done. Notes and sheets live beside the list.

    ## How to start
    Sign up at https://task.nevorai.com; Pro at billing.nevorai.com.

    ## What people use it for
    - Daily priorities
- Notes and small sheets
