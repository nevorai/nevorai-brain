# Nevorai Courses: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    Sell and deliver online courses on your own brand, with 0% commission on your sales.

    ## Who it is for
    Coaches, teachers, creators and institutes selling recorded or live courses.

    ## How it works
    Upload lessons, set prices, students pay into your own Razorpay, learn on web and phone.

    ## How to start
    See https://courses.nevorai.com; plans at billing.nevorai.com.

    ## What people use it for
    - Recorded courses with video and files
- Student progress and certificates
- A free website or landing page with the plan
