# Nevorai Connect: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    Your business WhatsApp number on the official WhatsApp Business API, with one shared inbox for the whole team, AI replies 24x7, broadcasts, templates, follow-ups that send themselves, lead stages, quotes and GST invoices sent in the chat, reminders, products, a booking link, and an API for your website.

    ## Who it is for
    Any business that gets leads and talks to customers on WhatsApp: coaching and academies, clinics, agencies, real estate, e-commerce, course creators, local services. Teams of 1 to 30.

    ## How it works
    You connect your WhatsApp Business number (a new number, or your existing one after moving it) in a few minutes. Messages arrive in the Connect inbox on phone and computer. The AI answers from your business profile, saved answers and Knowledge; your team takes over any chat with one tap. Follow-ups, broadcasts and lead scoring run on their own.

    ## How to start
    Sign up at https://connect.nevorai.com, pick a plan at billing.nevorai.com, then connect your number from Numbers (the Meta sign-in takes 5 minutes). Or reply DEMO and we do it with you on a call.

    ## What people use it for
    - Answer every lead in seconds, day and night
- One inbox so no lead is lost between team members
- Follow-ups to quiet leads without anyone remembering
- Broadcasts for offers and announcements to opted-in contacts
- Quotes and invoices sent in the chat, paid to the business's own UPI or bank
- Know which ad a lead came from and what it bought
