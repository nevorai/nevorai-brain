# Nevorai Connect: limits, known issues, what it does not do
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Limits by plan
Numbers, team members, websites and lead pages are per plan (see pricing.md). AI replies on Nevorai's key are capped per month by plan; Basic needs the AI top-up or your own Gemini key.

## Not built (do not promise)
Calendar sync with Google Calendar; an in-chat product catalog checkout; Instagram DMs; AI voice calls (planned as Nevorai Voice). Say "not yet, planned" and hand over if a customer needs it.

## Known behaviour
- A person replying in a chat pauses the AI there for 12 hours; the backup resumes it after 15 to 30 minutes of silence.
- A customer who replied STOP gets no automated messages.
- Template messages need Meta's approval, usually within minutes to a day.
