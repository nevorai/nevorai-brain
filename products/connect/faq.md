# Nevorai Connect: common questions
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Connect works in the phone browser; add it to the home screen and it behaves like an app. No laptop needed.

## Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number?
Yes. The number is moved to the WhatsApp Business API. During the move the normal WhatsApp app stops working on that number; chats move into the Connect inbox. We help you do it on the demo call.

## Can my whole team use it?
Yes. Basic has 5 team members, Growth 10, Pro 15, Scale 30. Extra seats are a top-up.

## Will the AI say wrong things?
It answers only from what you taught it and from your documents, and it hands the chat to your team when the answer is not there. You see every AI reply in the inbox and can fix it.

## Are WhatsApp messages free?
Replies inside 24 hours of a customer's message are free. Messages you start (templates, broadcasts, follow-ups outside 24 hours) are charged by Meta on your own Meta account. Nevorai adds nothing on top.

## Is there a free trial?
Trial days are set in Billing; the pricing page says the current offer. There are no refunds, so take the demo first.
