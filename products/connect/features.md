# Nevorai Connect: features
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Inbox
One shared team inbox for every WhatsApp number. Assign chats, notes, hand-over notes written by AI, resolve, snooze, "Needs you" flags, hot-lead badges, customer rating after a chat.

## AI replies
Answers 24x7 from the business profile, saved answers and Knowledge (PDFs, web pages, a public GitHub repo). Cites facts only; hands over when unsure. Fast or Smart model. Pauses for 12 hours when a person replies in a chat, and resumes within 15 to 30 minutes if that person then leaves the customer waiting.

## Follow-ups
Sequences that send themselves (text, voice note, template), start from a website, a form, a payment or a tag, stop on reply or purchase. Quiet hours.

## Broadcasts
Send a template to a saved audience (tags, stages, fields). Scheduled sends. Counts and delivery status.

## Lead stages and scores
Replied → Qualified → Booked → Bought (or Not interested, No-show). AI scores Hot, Warm, Cold and suggests stages; a person confirms Booked and Bought. Outcomes go back to the ad platform through AdsPro.

## Quotes and GST invoices
Create a GST quote or invoice in the chat; the PDF is sent on WhatsApp with a pay link or UPI details. Money goes to the business's own account. A customer link lets them accept, decline and pay.

## Products
A product list with pictures and prices; send a product card from any chat.

## Reminders
A to-do on a customer at a time; email when due.

## Voice notes
Record or pick a saved voice note and send it in the chat or in a follow-up.

## Bookings
A Bookings page inside Connect (Nevorai Bookings) shows the owner's next meetings.

## Website and forms
A chat button for your website, website enquiry forms into the inbox, lead pages.

## AI team (preview)
AI employees with one job each (receptionist, lead setter, follow-up, support, collections, back office), each switched on by the owner, starting in Suggest mode. See offers/ai-employees.md.

## Integrations

### Built in
Meta WhatsApp Business API (official), Razorpay pay links on quotes and invoices (the business's own account), Nevorai Bookings, Nevorai Landing Pages and Websites (leads into the inbox), AdsPro (lead outcomes back to Meta ads), Shopify (order and cart automations), Gmail for team emails, a website chat button and enquiry form, Knowledge from PDFs, web pages and a public GitHub repo.

### API
A Send API and an events API for developers (docs in the app under Connectors → Developers). Webhooks for new leads and outcomes.
