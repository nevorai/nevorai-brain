# Nevorai Connect: how to do things
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Connect a WhatsApp number
1. Open *Numbers* in the left menu. 2. Click *Connect a number*. 3. Sign in with the Facebook account that owns your business. 4. Pick or create the WhatsApp Business account and number. 5. Finish; the number shows as Connected.

## Turn AI replies on
1. Open *Automation* in the left menu. 2. Click *AI replies*. 3. Fill the business profile and saved answers. 4. Add documents under *Knowledge*. 5. Switch *AI replies* on. Use *Try it* to test.

## Send a broadcast
1. Open *Broadcasts*. 2. Click *New broadcast*. 3. Pick a template and an audience. 4. Send now or schedule.

## Create a follow-up
1. Open *Automation* then *Follow-ups*. 2. Click *New follow-up*. 3. Add steps (message, wait, voice note). 4. Choose what starts it and what stops it. 5. Switch it on.

## Send a quote or invoice
1. Open the chat. 2. Click *Quote* in the chat header. 3. Add items, GST and your payment details. 4. Send; the PDF goes in the chat.
