# Nevorai Flow: features
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Funnel steps
Video → lead form → testimonials → WhatsApp → code gate → landing page, in any order.

## Tracking
Who watched, how long, which step they reached; video view events per person.

## Leads
Lead form answers with the watch time; notify the creator by email and WhatsApp.

## Live sessions and landing pages
Schedule a live session with reminders; simple landing pages.

## Badge
A "Made with Flow" badge on lower plans.
## Integrations

### Works with
WhatsApp (click-to-chat button), Razorpay (plan payments), Meta Pixel on public pages, outbound webhooks for leads, Nevorai Connect (follow-ups from a Flow lead).
