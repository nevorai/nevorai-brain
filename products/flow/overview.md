# Nevorai Flow: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    Video funnels for people who sell by video: share one link, the prospect watches an unskippable video, fills a lead form, sees testimonials, and lands on WhatsApp or a landing page. You see exactly who watched and for how long.

    ## Who it is for
    Network-marketing leaders and distributors, coaches, course sellers, anyone who sends a video to prospects and then follows up on WhatsApp.

    ## How it works
    Create a funnel in minutes: upload or link a video, add steps (lead form, testimonials, WhatsApp button, code gate, landing page), share the link. Every view is tracked per person; leads arrive with their watch time; you follow up on WhatsApp.
