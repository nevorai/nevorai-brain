# Nevorai Academy: overview
    Last checked: 2026-11-07.

    ## What it is
    Management software for academies, sports academies, coaching institutes and gyms: students, attendance, fees, a parent app, coaches, matches, campaigns.

    ## Who it is for
    Academy, institute and gym owners with 50 to 5,000 students.

    ## How it works
    Add students and batches, mark attendance on phone, collect fees online into your own account, parents see everything in their app.

    ## How to start
    Ask the team for a demo; plans at billing.nevorai.com.

    ## What people use it for
    - Fee collection and reminders
- Attendance on phone
- Parent communication
