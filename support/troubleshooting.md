# Troubleshooting: the first things to try
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## "I can't log in"
1. Use the email you signed up with. 2. Request a new login link or OTP. 3. Check spam. 4. Still stuck: tell the team the email; a person fixes it in team hours.

## "My WhatsApp number shows Disconnected" (Connect)
1. Open *Numbers*. 2. Click *Reconnect*. 3. Sign in with the Facebook account that owns the business. If Meta shows a warning about the business, the team helps on a call.

## "The AI did not reply"
Check in this order: AI replies switched on (Automation → AI replies); the chat is not paused (a person replied there in the last 12 hours; the backup resumes it within 15 to 30 minutes); the customer wrote STOP earlier; the plan's monthly AI limit.

## "My template was rejected"
Meta rejects templates with promotional words in a utility category, or missing sample values. Edit and resubmit; the team can review it.

## "Payment done but plan not active"
Wait 2 minutes and reload. If still not active, send the payment email or Razorpay id to the team; it is fixed the same day.

## "Images or PDF not loading"
Reload once; if it stays, tell the team the page and the time; it is checked on the CDN.
