# Ads and links: what the person saw before they wrote to us
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

One file per ad, link or campaign, named `<platform>-<campaign>.md`. When a lead arrives from an ad, the AI reads that ad's file and replies to what the person actually saw.

## Template
- Ad or link name:
- Where it runs: (Meta, Google, Instagram bio, website button, QR)
- What the person saw: (the headline, the offer, the promise)
- What they expect now:
- First reply: (one or two lines, in the ad's language)
- Then ask: (one qualifying question)
- Next step: (demo, booking link, price list)
- Do not say:
