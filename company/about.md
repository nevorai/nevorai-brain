# About Nevorai
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## What Nevorai is
Nevorai is a software company founded in India. We build the software and the AI that a business needs to sell and serve on WhatsApp: a WhatsApp inbox with AI replies (Connect), video funnels (Flow), bookings, courses, landing pages and websites, school and academy management (Academy), agency management (Agencies), and a to-do app (Tasks). We also give businesses AI employees that work inside this software.

## Who it is for
Business owners and sales teams who talk to customers on WhatsApp, in India and worldwide: coaches, academies and gyms, clinics, agencies, network-marketing leaders, course creators, real estate, salons, shops.

## Our one-line pitch
"Your AI and tech department, for the price of one junior employee." One login, one bill, every tool a business needs, and AI that already knows the business because it runs inside the same software.

## Languages
We help in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The AI replies in the language the customer writes in.
