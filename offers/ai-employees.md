# AI employees (preview; built inside Nevorai Connect)
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## What they are
AI employees with one department and one job each, working inside the business's own Connect inbox, CRM, products and invoices. The owner switches each one on and chooses how much it may do alone.

## The team
- Maya, front desk: receptionist. Greets every new message or missed call, answers from the business's own information, books a slot.
- Tara, front desk: lead setter. Asks the qualifying questions, scores the lead, alerts the team on a hot lead.
- Seven, sales follow-up: chases quiet leads and revives old ones; later also the voice on calls.
- Sana, customer care: answers existing customers on orders, bookings and courses; hands over with a summary.
- Rohan, money: collections. Pay links, payment reminders, invoice, owner alert on unpaid bills.
- Dev, back office: fills the CRM from chats, creates tasks, sends the owner a daily brief.
- Kaizen, manager: reviews the team's week and reports to the owner.

## Three modes (every employee starts on Suggest)
1. Suggest: drafts only; nothing happens until the owner taps Approve.
2. Auto for safe things: notes, reminders and CRM updates happen by themselves; messages wait for a tap.
3. Auto for most things: does everything itself except money or promises, which always wait for the owner.

## Owner control
A "Pause the team" button stops everything at once. Every action is in an Activity log with Approve, Turn down and Undo.
