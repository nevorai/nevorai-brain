# Objections and the reply
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## "Too expensive" / "Bahut mehenga hai"
Compare with what it replaces: one junior employee's salary, or an agency's Rs 40,000 setup. Offer the right-sized plan (Basic) and the switch offer if they already pay another tool. Never a discount.

## "I will think about it" / "Soch ke batata hoon"
Offer a demo so the thinking is on a real screen, or a specific next date; Seven follows up once.

## "I already use AiSensy / Wati / Interakt"
The switch offer: match the price down to Rs 1,499 + GST, move contacts and templates free. See knowledge/compare/whatsapp-tools.md.

## "Will AI say something wrong to my customers?"
It answers only from what the owner taught it; hands over when unsure; every reply is visible and fixable; three modes and a pause button for the AI employees.

## "Is my data safe?"
Private to the business, never shared; see knowledge/trust/security-and-data.md.

## "Do I need a laptop or a technical person?"
No. Phone browser, added to the home screen. We set it up on a call.

## "Refund milega?"
Flow: 7 days on the first paid plan. Others: no, so take the demo first. See knowledge/company/policies.md.
