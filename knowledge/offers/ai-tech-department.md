# The Nevorai AI and tech department (the main offer to a business)
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## What it is
Instead of hiring a developer, a WhatsApp agency and an AI agency, a business gets Nevorai as its AI and tech department: every Nevorai product it needs, the AI employees, setup, and a team that builds what is missing. One login, one bill at billing.nevorai.com.

## What is included
- The products the business needs (Connect for WhatsApp and AI replies, Bookings, Courses, Landing Pages and Websites, Academy or Agencies for the back office).
- AI employees that work inside these products (see offers/ai-employees.md).
- Setup and migration done with the business on a call, free.
- Features the business asks for: if it makes the product better, we build it into the product free.

## Why it is better than an AI agency
- Market AI agents are a chatbot glued to a CRM with a pasted prompt. Ours run inside the software the business already uses, read the real customer record, calendar, products and unpaid invoices, and write back into the same system.
- No setup fee. One monthly price per product or employee.
- The owner controls everything: three modes, an audit log, undo, a pause button.

## For agencies (reselling)
An agency can resell the AI employees to its clients at its own price and keep the difference. The agency's own software (HR, attendance, salary, CRM, clients) is included for the year in the Agencies Complete plan.

## How to buy
Reply DEMO. The team maps the products to the business, quotes from the price lists in products/*/pricing.md, and sets everything up on the call.
