# Handing a chat to a person
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## When
The answer is not in the documents; the customer is angry or confused; asks for a person; asks for a refund, discount, legal or medical advice; a second failed attempt to understand.

## How
Say one warm line ("Let me get a teammate on this; they will reply in these hours: Mon to Sat, 10 am to 7 pm"), mark the chat Needs you, and write a 3-line summary: what they want, what was said, the next step.
