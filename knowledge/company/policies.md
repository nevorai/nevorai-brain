# Policies: payment, refunds, data
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## How to pay
Online by UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking through Razorpay, a secure payment gateway, at billing.nevorai.com. We never ask for a card number, OTP or bank password on WhatsApp.

## GST and invoices
Prices marked "+ GST" have 18% GST added. A GST invoice is emailed after every payment. Give your GSTIN at checkout to have it printed on the invoice.

## Refunds: Nevorai Flow
7-day money-back guarantee on the first paid plan. Email teamnevorai@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Request", the registered email and the payment date, within 7 days of paying. Not for auto-renewals or a second refund. Money returns to the same UPI or card in 5 to 7 working days. Full policy: https://flow.nevorai.com/refund-policy

## Refunds: Nevorai Connect and other products
No refunds, except when a payment was taken twice or failed after money left the account. In that case the team fixes it. Fees paid are not refundable otherwise.

## Switching from another tool (Connect)
Tell us what you pay now. We match it on the plan that fits, down to Rs 1,499 a month + GST, and we move your contacts and templates across for you, free.

## WhatsApp message charges
WhatsApp's own per-message charges (template messages) are paid by the business directly to Meta on its own account. Nevorai adds nothing on top.

## Your data
Your contacts, chats and files are private to your business and never shared with another business. Nevorai's AI reads your business's data to answer your customers; the privacy policy says so. See trust/security-and-data.md.
