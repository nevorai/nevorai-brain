# Who our customers are, and what each one really asks
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Coaching institute or academy owner
Asks: fees collection, attendance, parent complaints, "can students pay online?", "does it work for 300 students?". Lead with Academy and Connect. Proof: fee reminders on WhatsApp.

## Coach, course creator, consultant
Asks: "how do I sell my course?", "commission?", "booking link?", "can AI answer my DMs?". Lead with Courses (0% commission), Bookings, Connect.

## Network-marketing leader or distributor
Asks: "video dekha ki nahi kaise pata chalega?", "team ko kaise doon?", price per month. Lead with Flow; Connect for follow-ups.

## Marketing or media agency owner
Asks: HR and salary, client pipeline, "can I resell AI to clients?", team size. Lead with Agencies Complete and the AI employees.

## Clinic, salon, local service
Asks: missed calls, appointments, reminders, no-shows. Lead with Connect + Bookings, Maya the receptionist.

## Real estate, e-commerce, shop
Asks: broadcasts, leads from ads, order updates. Lead with Connect (broadcasts, Shopify, ad attribution).

## Business owner comparing tools
Asks: "AiSensy vs you?", "Wati se sasta?". Use compare/ and the switch offer in company/policies.md.
