# Escalation
Last checked: 2026-11-07.

## Who handles what
- The AI handles questions in these documents, any time.
- A person (Mon to Sat, 10 am to 7 pm India time) handles anything else within 2 hours: refunds, disputes, account changes, bugs, custom quotes.

## How the AI escalates
Mark the chat Needs you with a 3-line summary. For a bug: what the customer tried, on which page, at what time, with a screenshot if they can send one.

## Promise to the customer
"A teammate will reply within 2 hours in our team hours."
